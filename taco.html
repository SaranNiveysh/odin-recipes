Taco Recipe

Ingredients:
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 packet taco seasoning
  • 8 taco shells
  • 1 cup shredded lettuce
  • 1 cup diced tomatoes
  • 1 cup shredded cheese
  • Sour cream (optional)
Instructions:
  1. In a skillet, cook the ground beef over medium heat until browned. Drain any excess fat.
  2. Add the taco seasoning and water according to the packet instructions. Simmer for 5 minutes.
  3. Warm the taco shells according to the package instructions.
  4. Assemble the tacos by filling each shell with the cooked beef, lettuce, tomatoes, cheese, and any other desired toppings.
  5. Serve immediately and enjoy!
